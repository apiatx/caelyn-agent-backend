## New canonical Quality fields

Before creating a field, search the entire live backend for an existing equivalent. Reuse the existing canonical value and source if one already exists.

### Financial Strength

Add or reuse:

* `Cash`

  * Cash and cash equivalents plus short-term investments when available.
  * Document the exact components.

* `Net Cash / Debt`

  * Cash plus short-term investments minus total debt.
  * Positive means net cash.
  * Negative means net debt.

* `Current Ratio`

  * Prefer a confirmed direct TTM FMP value.
  * Otherwise calculate current assets divided by current liabilities.

* `Interest Coverage`

  * Prefer a confirmed direct field.
  * Otherwise TTM EBIT divided by absolute TTM interest expense.
  * Null/not meaningful for invalid denominators.

* `Cash Runway Months`

* `Cash Runway Status`

  * Negative TTM FCF:
    cash divided by absolute TTM FCF multiplied by 12.
  * Positive or zero TTM FCF:
    status `self_funding`; months may remain null.
  * Banks, insurers, and inappropriate financial businesses:
    status `not_meaningful`.
  * Never use zero to represent missing data.

* `Altman Z-Score`

* `Altman Z-Risk`

  * Prefer the entitled FMP Financial Scores result.
  * Do not silently substitute another score.
  * Mark industries where it is not economically appropriate.

### Business Quality

Add or reuse:

* `ROIC`
* `Operating Margin`
* `FCF Conversion`
* `FCF Yield`
* `Diluted Shares Growth YoY`
* `SBC / Revenue`

Definitions:

* Operating Margin = TTM operating income / TTM revenue
* FCF Conversion = TTM free cash flow / TTM net income
* FCF Yield = TTM free cash flow / canonical market capitalization
* Diluted Shares Growth YoY = current TTM weighted-average diluted shares versus prior TTM
* SBC / Revenue = TTM stock-based compensation / TTM revenue

Rules:

* FCF Conversion is not meaningful when net income is zero, negative, or immaterial.
* Do not assume missing SBC equals zero.
* Use denominator safeguards against absurd values.

### Growth Quality

Add or reuse:

* `Revenue Acceleration`
* `Gross Margin Change YoY`
* `Incremental Operating Margin`
* `Forward Revenue Growth`
* `Revenue Estimate Revision 90D`
* `EPS Estimate Revision 90D`

Definitions:

* Revenue Acceleration =
  latest quarterly YoY revenue growth minus previous quarter’s YoY growth

* Gross Margin Change YoY =
  current TTM gross margin minus prior-year TTM gross margin, expressed in percentage points

* Incremental Operating Margin =
  change in TTM operating income divided by change in TTM revenue

* Forward Revenue Growth =
  FY1 consensus revenue divided by latest actual fiscal-year revenue minus one

Rules:

* Match periods using fiscal year, fiscal quarter, and reported period.
* Do not rely solely on array positions.
* Incremental margin is not meaningful when revenue change is zero, negative, or too small.
* Clearly identify FY1 versus NTM in metadata.

### Valuation

Reuse these existing fields unchanged:

* `PE Ratio`
* `PS Ratio`
* `EV/EBITDA`
* `Forward P/E`

Add or reuse:

* `Forward P/S`
* `Forward EV/Sales`
* `Forward EV/EBITDA`
* `P/FCF`

Definitions:

* Forward P/S = canonical market cap / FY1 consensus revenue
* Forward EV/Sales = canonical enterprise value / FY1 consensus revenue
* Forward EV/EBITDA = canonical enterprise value / FY1 consensus EBITDA
* P/FCF = canonical market cap / TTM free cash flow

Rules:

* Negative or zero FCF makes P/FCF not meaningful.
* Negative or zero forward EBITDA makes Forward EV/EBITDA not meaningful.
* Use the existing canonical market-cap resolver where appropriate.
* Audit the existing enterprise-value source before deriving another one.
* Do not make request-time FMP or quote calls.
