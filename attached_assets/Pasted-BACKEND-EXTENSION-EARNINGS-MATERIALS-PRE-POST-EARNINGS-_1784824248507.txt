PART 1 — PRE/POST EARNINGS PRICE MOVES

Extend every earnings_history record’s price_reaction object.

Add:

- pre_earnings_1d_pct
- post_earnings_1d_pct
- pre_earnings_session
- post_earnings_session
- pre_post_method
- pre_post_confidence

Use actual trading sessions from canonical_history_service.

For AMC earnings:

- pre_earnings_session = report-date regular session
- prior_session = immediately preceding trading session
- pre_earnings_1d_pct =
  report-date close / prior-session close - 1

- post_earnings_session = next trading session
- post_earnings_1d_pct =
  next-session close / report-date close - 1

For BMO earnings:

- pre_earnings_session = immediately preceding trading session
- prior_prior_session = trading session before that
- pre_earnings_1d_pct =
  prior-session close / prior-prior-session close - 1

- post_earnings_session = report-date session
- post_earnings_1d_pct =
  report-date close / prior-session close - 1

The existing reaction_1d_pct should normally equal post_earnings_1d_pct for sufficiently classified BMO/AMC events.

For timing=unknown or timing_confidence=inferred_low:

- do not claim exact release-relative precision
- return null when the pre/post session cannot be established safely
- or return a clearly labeled approximation
- pre_post_method must make the methodology explicit
- never silently assume AMC

Percentages remain percentage-point values:

6.25 means 6.25%, not 0.0625%.

PART 2 — PRE/POST SUMMARY STATISTICS

- continuation_count
- reversal_count
- continuation_rate
- reversal_rate
- average_post_after_positive_pre
- average_post_after_negative_pre

Definitions:

Continuation:
- pre and post are both positive, or
- pre and post are both negative

Reversal:
- pre and post have opposite signs

Zero or null observations should not be forced into either group.

Use only observations with methodologically valid pre and post values.
