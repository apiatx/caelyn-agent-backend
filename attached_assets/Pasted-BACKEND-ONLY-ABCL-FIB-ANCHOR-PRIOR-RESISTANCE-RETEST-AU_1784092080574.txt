PART 5 — PROPOSE GENERAL RULE
==================================================

Do not implement yet unless the rule is obviously safe.

Propose a general rule:

S2_BREAKOUT_FLOOR_RETEST

Possible requirements:

- stage_label in S2/S2-S3/S3 Momentum
- primary_fib_context exists
- wave_structure_label in SECOND_LEG_CONTINUATION / MOMENTUM_WAVE_PULLBACK
- current price within <=5% of Fib 1.0 or prior resistance shelf
- price pulled back at least 8–10% from recent high
- price still above shelf or reclaimed it
- extension_quality != BROKEN

- risk_flags do not include LOWER_LOW_CONFIRMED / MAJOR_LOWER_LOW / RISK_CONFLICT

If close to shelf and holding:
entry_state = PRIOR_RESISTANCE_RETEST
execution_state = RETEST_IN_PROGRESS
