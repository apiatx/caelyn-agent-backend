Examples:

* fed_rate_decision +81.1pp because June FOMC rotated to July FOMC
* jobs_unemployment +45.9pp because monthly question rotated
* bitcoin_price +86.5pp because question/date rotated
* israel_gaza -42.7pp because matched question changed

These should not be treated as real 24h / 7d odds moves.

Requirement:
Apply the same market-identity discipline to Polymarket deltas that was applied to Kalshi daily deltas.

Rules:

1. For delta calculation, compare only the same underlying market identity:

   * Polymarket: condition_id / market_id / slug, whichever is stable and unique for the contract.
   * Kalshi: market_ticker.
2. If the family_key is the same but the underlying market changed, do not compute a family-level delta.
3. Return:

   * price_change_24h: null
   * price_change_7d: null
   * delta_missing_reason: "new_contract_or_rotated_market"
     or equivalent diagnostic.
4. Do not show June → July Fed rotation as +81.1pp.
5. Do not show old BTC date → new BTC date as +86.5pp.
6. Do not show changed geopolitical question as a real Top Mover.
7. Long-lived identical markets should still compute normal deltas.
8. Multi-outcome families should compare the same event/condition where possible.
9. If a market rotates but the family remains active, keep the row; just suppress bogus deltas.
