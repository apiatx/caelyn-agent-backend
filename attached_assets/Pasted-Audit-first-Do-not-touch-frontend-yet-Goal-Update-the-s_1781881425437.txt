Required label taxonomy:
Use these exact labels:

S1 Base:

* bottoming/base-building
* price below or near long-term trend
* not yet confirmed breakout

S1-2 Watch:

* base tightening
* price reclaiming key moving averages
* near breakout / support-to-breakout setup
* early constructive setup, but not confirmed breakout yet

S2 Breakout:

* confirmed breakout / early Stage 2
* price above key moving averages
* breakout from base or strong reclaim
* trend beginning to expand

S2-S3 Advance:

* established advance
* price above rising long-term moving average
* trend still healthy
* not just breaking out; already in the advance phase

S3 Momentum:

* mature momentum / late-stage trend
* price may be extended
* momentum is still strong, but risk is rising
* should not be classified as S2 Breakout if it is already far into the move

S3-S4 Top:

* topping / distribution risk
* momentum weakening
* price losing structure or failing near highs
* not yet confirmed Stage 4 decline

S4 Decline:

* confirmed downtrend
* below declining long-term trend
* weak structure
