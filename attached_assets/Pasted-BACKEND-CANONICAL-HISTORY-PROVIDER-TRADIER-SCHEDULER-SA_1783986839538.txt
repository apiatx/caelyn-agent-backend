BACKEND — CANONICAL HISTORY PROVIDER + TRADIER SCHEDULER SAFETY AUDIT

Backend only.

Do not touch frontend.
Do not change Confluence weights.
Do not hardcode ticker symbols.
Do not call LLMs.
Do not add request-time provider calls.
Do not bypass the existing Tradier scheduler / limiter.
Do not break Options Flow.
Do not break retained snapshot rebuilds.

Goal:

Tradier may now support full historical daily candles through /v1/markets/history using start/end dates. If true, Tradier can be the primary canonical historical price provider, with FMP as fallback/verification.

But Tradier is also heavily used for:
- options flow
- quotes
- rel volume
- options chains
- watchlist/portfolio market data

The risk is not a one-time cached historical backfill. The risk is the new historical job bypassing the existing Tradier pacing/scheduler or starving options flow.

We need to verify provider capability and make canonical history a low-priority scheduled/backfill lane that respects the existing Tradier rate-limit system.

==================================================
PART 5 — INTEGRATE HISTORY BACKFILL INTO TRADIER SCHEDULER
==================================================

Canonical history backfill must be a low-priority lane inside the existing Tradier scheduler.

Add lane:

canonical_history_backfill

Rules:

- lowest priority
- never bypass global Tradier limiter
- no direct raw HTTP calls
- pause or throttle if options_flow queue is active
- pause or throttle if market is open and options jobs are behind
- prefer off-hours / weekend
- resumable
- cache-first
- skip fresh symbols
- retry with backoff
- save progress after every symbol
- safe to stop/restart

Suggested safe lane behavior:

During market hours:
- canonical_history_backfill RPM = 0–5 max
- only run if options queue is idle

Off-hours:
- canonical_history_backfill RPM = 10–30 max
- still respect global limiter
- leave reserved headroom for quotes/options maintenance

Weekend:
- canonical_history_backfill can run more aggressively but still below global limiter

Do not hardcode exact RPM if the existing scheduler already has config.
Use scheduler config/env where possible.

Expose diagnostics:

history_lane_rpm_config
history_lane_current_usage
history_lane_paused_reason
history_lane_backlog_count
history_lane_last_symbol
history_lane_last_success_at
history_lane_last_error
tradier_global_available_tokens
options_flow_queue_depth
quotes_queue_depth

==================================================
PART 6 — ONE-TIME BACKFILL VS DAILY MAINTENANCE
==================================================

The initial 5-year backfill should happen once per eligible symbol.

After that:

- do not refetch 5 years every day
- append only missing daily bars after market close
- skip weekends/holidays when no new bar exists
- full 5-year refresh only monthly or manual/admin
- if cache corrupt/missing, re-backfill that symbol

Add:

canonical_history_refresh_mode

Allowed values:

initial_full_backfill
incremental_daily_append
weekly_health_check
monthly_full_refresh
manual_rebuild
cache_read_only

Tier 1:
