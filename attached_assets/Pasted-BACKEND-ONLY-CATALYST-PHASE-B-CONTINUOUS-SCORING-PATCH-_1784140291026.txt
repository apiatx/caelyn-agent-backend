PART 2 — IMPLEMENT GRADUATED CATALYST DIRECT SCORE
==================================================

Replace binary event scoring with event-tier scoring.

Suggested direct catalyst quality score, 0–100:

Tier A — 85–100:
- FDA/regulatory decision/readout
- confirmed major contract/customer win
- hyperscaler/strategic customer deal
- earnings guidance raise
- material M&A
- major government/defense award
- confirmed product launch with direct company impact

Tier B — 65–85:
- earnings date / earnings report proximity
- analyst upgrade / price target raise if cached
- partnership
- investor day / analyst day
- product update
- sector-specific event with direct ticker relevance

Tier C — 40–65:
- commercial contract mention with unclear size
- generic business update
- non-material partnership
- thematic/sector tailwind directly tied to ticker

Tier D — 15–40:
- generic RSS mention
- theme_policy-only item
- weak/undated event
- sector-wide news with indirect relevance

Tier E — 0–15:
- stale, generic, low-confidence, or no direct ticker catalyst

Bearish conflict:
- if bearish_conflict true, catalyst_points should be 0 or strongly capped, preserving current behavior.

Important:
rss_v2 alone should NOT mean 100.
commercial_contract should NOT automatically equal M&A or earnings.
unknown event type should not score above 40 unless strong cat_score/confidence exists.
