====================

The user manually reviewed real charts and identified two concrete failures:

The pullback tested an ascending trendline formed by prior meaningful swing lows. The daily structure remained intact at the time of classification.

* major prior advance
* consolidation near the highs
* defined horizontal resistance/ceiling
* repeated pressure beneath resistance
* higher lows
* controlled or contracting range
* price coiling toward the ceiling
* breakout above the range has NOT yet been materially confirmed

These are structural test cases.

Do not hardcode SLAB, ALGM, or DELL.

The goal is to correct the deterministic chart geometry.

The problem is NOT insufficient intraday granularity.

The DELL and ALGM structures are clearly visible on DAILY charts.

---

## HIGH_BASE_COILING

Use when:

base is established
price remains near highs
range is contracting or controlled

## HIGH_BASE_READY

Use when:

HIGH_BASE_COILING conditions are met

AND

price is near the base ceiling

AND

distance_to_base_high_pct is small

AND

range is tight/controlled

AND

resistance has been tested multiple times or is otherwise clearly defined

AND

price has NOT materially confirmed a breakout above the base

This is the intended DELL-type setup.

PART 8 — CLASSIFICATION PRECEDENCE
==================================

Review and explicitly define Entry State precedence.

The intended structural precedence is:

1. INSUFFICIENT_DATA

2. STRUCTURALLY_BROKEN

   * confirmed failed breakout
   * broken major structural support
   * confirmed downtrend

3. EXTREME_CHASE / CLIMAX

   * extreme vertical move
   * volume climax
   * extreme extension with no base

4. POST_BREAKOUT STRUCTURE

   * BREAKOUT_CONFIRMED
   * WAIT_FOR_RETEST
   * BREAKOUT_RETEST

5. STRUCTURAL SUPPORT / CONTINUATION

   * TRENDLINE_SUPPORT_TEST
   * CONSTRUCTIVE_DIP
   * PULLBACK_IN_UPTREND
   * SUPPORT_HOLD
   * REACCELERATION

6. PRE-BREAKOUT HIGH BASE

   * HIGH_BASE_READY
   * HIGH_BASE_COILING
   * HIGH_BASE_FORMING

7. EARLY / PRE-MOVE

   * COILED
   * BASE_FORMING
   * EARLY_ACCUMULATION
   * SIGNALS_BUILDING
   * BREAKOUT_READY

8. NO_CLEAR_ENTRY

Do not allow a generic fresh_breakout signal to override:

confirmed post-breakout extension

or:

already-cleared base ceiling

Do not allow a generic failed_breakout signal to override:

valid ascending structural support

unless true breakout-pivot failure is confirmed.
