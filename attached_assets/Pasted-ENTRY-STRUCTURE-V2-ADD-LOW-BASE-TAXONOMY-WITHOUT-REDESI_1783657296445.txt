PRODUCT DEFINITION
==================

The user wants to recognize TWO different bullish base archetypes.

---

## HIGH BASE

Concept:

A prior leader / strong uptrend has advanced materially.

Price then consolidates in the upper region of its prior move or broader range.

Momentum and leadership have already been demonstrated.

The base represents:

continuation

compression

another potential leg higher

Trade interpretation:

"PROVEN MOMENTUM LEADER RESETTING FOR CONTINUATION"

Current canonical states:

HIGH_BASE_FORMING

HIGH_BASE_COILING

HIGH_BASE_READY

These states must remain.

---

## LOW BASE

Concept:

A stock has experienced a substantial prior decline, prolonged reset, or major drawdown.

Price stops making meaningful downside progress.

A repeatable structural floor/support area develops.

The range begins to stabilize and/or contract.

Downside becomes increasingly defined.

Momentum has NOT necessarily been proven yet.

The base may require more patience.

The trade represents:

asymmetric turnaround / early reversal potential

rather than momentum continuation.

Trade interpretation:

"DOWNSIDE DEFINED, MOMENTUM UNPROVEN, ASYMMETRIC TURN POTENTIALLY FORMING"

Create canonical states:

LOW_BASE_FORMING

LOW_BASE_COILING

LOW_BASE_READY

Do not use generic BUY/SELL labels.

---

## LOW_BASE INTENT

A LOW_BASE should require evidence of:

meaningful prior decline / drawdown / prolonged reset

base located in lower region of meaningful prior range

defined horizontal or clustered support/floor

multiple support interactions where detectable

downside progress slowing

range stabilizing and/or contracting

no current decisive structural breakdown

The stock does NOT need proven positive momentum.

The stock does NOT need to be near a 52-week high.

The stock does NOT need Stage 2.

Do not classify every oversold stock as a LOW_BASE.

A falling knife is not a base.

A stock making persistent lower lows is not a base.

A one-day bounce is not a base.

Require actual daily structural stabilization.

---

## MID_RANGE_BASE

Do not force MID_RANGE_BASE into the new LOW_BASE states.

PART 4 — LOW BASE FORMING / COILING / READY
===========================================

Create:

LOW_BASE_FORMING

Intent:

A legitimate lower-region base/floor is developing.

Downside deterioration has slowed.

Support exists but structure remains immature, wide, or inconsistent.

Momentum is not proven.

Typical characteristics:

valid low-base archetype

floor evidence present

base duration sufficient

but:

range still relatively loose

or:

few support confirmations

or:

no meaningful contraction / reclaim pressure yet

---

## LOW_BASE_COILING

Intent:

A mature lower-region base is tightening.

Downside remains defined.

Range contraction / higher lows / pressure toward an internal resistance or reclaim level is developing.

Momentum still may not be proven.

Potential evidence:

mature floor

multiple floor tests

range contraction

higher lows or improving lows

decreasing volatility

price migrating toward upper region of the low-base range

Do not require a breakout.

---

## LOW_BASE_READY

Intent:

A mature low base has a well-defined floor and a near-term structural trigger is forming.

The trade has attractive structural asymmetry:

limited downside to defined support

meaningful upside if the early reversal/reclaim succeeds

Momentum is not necessarily proven, so this is DIFFERENT from HIGH_BASE_READY.

Potential evidence:

mature low base

strong floor quality

tightening range

higher lows / accumulation-like compression

price pressing internal base ceiling or reclaim pivot

and/or

recent undercut-reclaim with floor intact

Do not require Stage 2.

Do not require strong prior positive momentum.

Do not label a low base READY solely because price is close to support.

Dead money at support is not automatically ready.
