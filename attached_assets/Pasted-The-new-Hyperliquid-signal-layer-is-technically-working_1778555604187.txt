The new Hyperliquid signal layer is technically working, but the signal classification is too conservative and too punitive.

Problem:
- It is only producing a tiny number of LONG signals across the whole market.
- Many potentially good trades are being labeled AVOID or CROWDED.
- Vast majority of rows are NEUTRAL or low-scored.
- Missing/warming OI delta or volume velocity appears to be hurting scores/signals too much.
- Funding/crowding/thin labels seem to override directional opportunity too aggressively.

Do NOT roll back the backend.
Do NOT remove the new fields.
Do NOT break existing raw fields or cards.
This is a calibration fix to the signal logic only.

Goal:
Make the screener surface actionable candidates first, then show risk separately.

Core principle:
Signal direction and risk must be separated.

matrixSignal should primarily be:
- LONG
- SHORT
- WATCH
- NEUTRAL
- AVOID only for severe untradeable conditions

CROWDED should usually be a riskLabel, not the main matrixSignal.

Examples:
Good:
matrixSignal: LONG
riskLabel: CROWDED
matrixReason: "Price + OI building, funding hot"

Bad:
matrixSignal: CROWDED
riskLabel: CROWDED

Good:
matrixSignal: WATCH
riskLabel: MED
matrixReason: "Momentum positive, OI/volume warming"

Bad:
matrixSignal: NEUTRAL
matrixReason: "No clear signal" just because OI delta is null

Calibration tasks:

1. Treat missing/warming OI delta and volume velocity as UNKNOWN, not bearish.
If oi_delta_15m_pct or volume_velocity_15m is null because snapshots are warming:
- Do not score it as zero.
- Do not penalize opportunity_score heavily.
- Use available signals: 24h momentum, relative strength, existing composite signal, trade imbalance, book imbalance, funding, premium, volume, existing breakout/agent score.
- If other signals are constructive, classify as WATCH or LONG even while OI/Vol fields are warming.

2. Lower LONG threshold.
Current LONG logic is too strict.

New suggested LONG condition:
matrixSignal = LONG if:
- opportunity_score >= 0.45
AND at least two constructive inputs are present:
  - positive 24h change
  - positive existing signalDirection / side / compositeSignal
  - positive relative strength / time-series momentum
  - OI delta positive when available
  - volume velocity > 1.2 when available
  - buy/positive trade imbalance
  - positive/supportive book imbalance
  - flowLabel is Building, Buying, Squeeze, or Absorbing
  - funding is not extreme-dislocation

Funding hot should increase risk, but should not automatically prevent LONG unless truly extreme.

3. Lower WATCH threshold.
matrixSignal = WATCH if:
- opportunity_score >= 0.32
OR partial constructive inputs exist
OR OI/volume history is warming but price/momentum/flow is positive
OR current existing logic would have been breakout/bullish/long-ish

WATCH should be common. It means “interesting but not confirmed.”

4. Make AVOID rare.
matrixSignal = AVOID only if:
- severe cap risk
- severe thin liquidity
- extreme mark/oracle dislocation
- broken/missing core market data
- risk_score >= 0.85 AND opportunity_score < 0.45
- flowLabel is Thin AND liquidity is truly below hard threshold

Do NOT mark AVOID merely because:
- funding is elevated
- riskLabel is MED/HIGH
- OI delta is missing
- volume velocity is missing
- flowLabel is Mixed
- liquidity is smaller but still tradeable

5. Move CROWDED out of matrixSignal in most cases.
If a setup is bullish but crowded:
- matrixSignal should be LONG or WATCH
- riskLabel should be CROWDED
- riskReason should mention funding/premium/OI crowding

Only set matrixSignal = CROWDED if:
- risk_score >= 0.80
- funding/premium is extreme
- opportunity_score is not strong enough to justify LONG/WATCH
- or price is already extended and reversal risk dominates

6. Rebalance opportunity_score.
Current scores are too compressed around 0.3–0.5.

Recalibrate opportunity_score so strong candidates can reach 0.65–0.90.

Suggested:
- Normalize each component to 0–1.
- Missing components should be excluded from denominator, not counted as zero.
- Existing strong signals should matter more.

Suggested weights:
- 20% price / relative strength / time-series momentum
- 20% OI delta when available
- 15% volume velocity when available
- 15% trade/book flow
- 15% existing composite/agent/breakout signal
- 10% funding quality
- 5% liquidity/tradability

If OI delta or volume velocity are unavailable, redistribute that weight across available components instead of penalizing.

7. Recalibrate risk_score.
Risk should warn, not suppress.

Risk labels:
- LOW: risk_score < 0.35
- MED: 0.35–0.60
- HIGH: 0.60–0.78
- CROWDED: funding/premium crowding or risk_score >= 0.70 with bullish setup
- CAP RISK: actual cap utilization issue
- THIN: actual liquidity issue

Do not let riskLabel override matrixSignal except for severe AVOID cases.

8. Use existing old logic as fallback.
The old fields were useful and should influence the new matrixSignal:
- signalDirection
- compositeSignal
- overallScore
- agentScore
- breakout score
- time-series momentum
- relative strength
- vol impulse
- short squeeze module
- order book pressure

If the old logic says a row is attractive but new OI/Vol fields are warming, classify at least WATCH, possibly LONG.

9. Expected output distribution.
After calibration, on a 600+ asset universe, it is reasonable to see something like:
- LONG: 5–15% of assets
- SHORT: 3–10%
- WATCH: 15–30%
- NEUTRAL: 40–65%
- AVOID: under 5–8%
- CROWDED as main matrixSignal: rare, preferably under 3–5%

CROWDED should more often appear as riskLabel, not matrixSignal.

10. Validation.
After changes, validate live endpoint distribution:
- Count matrixSignal values across all assets.
- Count riskLabel values.
- Show top 20 by opportunityScore.
- Confirm multiple LONG and WATCH rows exist.
- Confirm AVOID is rare.
- Confirm OI/volume warming does not cause mass NEUTRAL/AVOID.
- Confirm old fields still exist.
