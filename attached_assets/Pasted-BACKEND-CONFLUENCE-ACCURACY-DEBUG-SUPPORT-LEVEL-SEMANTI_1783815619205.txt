PART 2 — SUPPORT SEMANTICS REPAIR
==================================================

Audit current Entry logic for SUPPORT_LOST / FAILED_BREAKOUT.

The current behavior is wrong if a ticker is merely sitting on or testing a major support zone.

Implement or expose support hierarchy:

1. major support:
   base low
   prior breakout pivot
   major horizontal cluster
   200DMA / long MA zone if relevant
   weekly swing low

2. minor support:
   short-term moving average
   small pivot
   recent micro swing

SUPPORT_LOST should require:

- close below major support by meaningful buffer
- confirmation close or lower-low confirmation
- not merely touching / slightly under a minor level

SUPPORT_TEST should mean:

- price is within support zone
- support is not confirmed broken

LOWER_HIGH_WARNING should mean:

- lower high likely formed
- but lower low not confirmed

LOWER_LOW_CONFIRMED should mean:

- current structure has taken out prior meaningful swing low

FAILED_BREAKOUT should require:

- prior breakout above pivot
- then confirmed failure below breakout pivot / base support
- not just normal retest
