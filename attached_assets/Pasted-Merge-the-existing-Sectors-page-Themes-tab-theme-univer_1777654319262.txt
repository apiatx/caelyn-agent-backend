Step 3 — Dedupe rules
Do not remove useful old themes. Only merge true duplicates.

Use this logic:
- If old and new themes are the same concept, merge into one theme_id.
- Preserve all proxy ETFs from both old and new versions.
- Preserve better display names where possible.
- Preserve candidate/dynamic discovery behavior.
- Do not show duplicate rows for the same concept.

Examples:
- “Uranium & Nuclear Energy” and “Nuclear & Uranium” should become one theme.
  Proxies: URA, URNM, NLR.
- “Defense” and “Aerospace & Defense” should become one theme unless there is a strong reason to keep both.
  Proxies: ITA, XAR, PPA.
- “Copper & Industrial Metals” and “Copper Miners” may be merged only if they are truly the same. If one is broader industrial metals and one is miners, keep both or make one parent/subtheme if the schema supports it.
- “Lithium & Batteries” and “Lithium & Battery Tech” should become one theme.
  Proxies: LIT, BATT.
- “Cybersecurity” should be one theme.
  Proxies: CIBR, HACK, BUG, IHAK.
- “Robotics & Automation” should be one theme.
  Proxies: BOTZ, ROBO, IRBO.
- “Regional Banks” should be one theme.
  Proxies: KRE, KBE.

  Proxies: XBI, IBB, ARKG.
