CONCERN 2 — provider_partial MUST NOT MEAN "HISTORY STILL WARMING"

The current report says:

no first_seen → warming
47h59m → warming
48h exactly → provider_partial
72h → provider_partial
95h59m → provider_partial
96h exactly → complete

This conflates collector-history warm-up with provider failure.

Required semantics:

warming:
The ticker has not yet accumulated the full 96 hours of collector observation history required for current 48H vs previous 48H comparison.

provider_partial:
Yahoo or Google collection coverage is genuinely incomplete/failed for the relevant current collector state.

stale:
Collector state or ticker refresh is outside the existing freshness threshold.

complete:
At least 96 hours of observation history exists and current provider/freshness coverage is healthy.

Therefore, for a ticker with healthy Yahoo AND Google collection:

0h through 95h59m59s
→ warming

96h+
→ complete

A healthy ticker at 48h or 72h must NOT return provider_partial merely because comparison history is not complete yet.

In that case use:

age < 96h → warming
age >= 96h and fresh → complete
stale according to current existing freshness rule → stale

Preserve provider_partial only where the collector actually has evidence of partial Yahoo/Google coverage.
