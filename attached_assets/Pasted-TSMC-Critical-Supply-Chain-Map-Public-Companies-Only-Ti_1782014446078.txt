Tier 4: CoWoS / Advanced Packaging / 3DFabric

This is the new sexy bottleneck. TSMC’s CoWoS-S uses a silicon interposer for ultra-high-performance AI/supercomputing packages, integrating logic chiplets with HBM cubes. Reuters also describes CoWoS as TSMC’s advanced packaging technology widely used for AI chips, including Nvidia-designed chips. And TSMC is still emphasizing that wafer-level CoWoS has more near-term scaling runway than panel-level packaging.

Best Bottleneck Buckets for Alpha
1. Highest-quality compounders

ASML, AMAT, LRCX, KLAC, TEL, ASMI, SNPS, CDNS

These are elite businesses, but many are already institutionally owned, well understood, and often expensive. Great long-term names. Not always asymmetric from here.

2. Best “boring but critical” names

SCREEN, Ebara, Kokusai, ULVAC, JX Advanced Metals, Resonac, Merck, Entegris, Tokyo Ohka, Shin-Etsu, SUMCO

This is the classic pick-and-shovel layer. Less memeable, more durable.

3. Best smaller-player hunting ground

Taiwan Puritic, Marketech, Scientech, Jentech, Topco Scientific, All Ring, C Sun, United Integrated Services, Organo, Technoprobe, Shibaura Mechatronics

This is where the asymmetric work should focus next. The downside: liquidity, Taiwan/Japan disclosure friction, and brutal chart volatility. The upside: these are not as “fully processed” by U.S. investors.

4. Best CoWoS / advanced packaging basket

DISCO, Onto, Advantest, Shibaura, Asahi Kasei, Unimicron, Ibiden, Nan Ya PCB, Kinsus, Ajinomoto, Technoprobe, Amkor, ASE

This is probably the hottest sub-chain tied to TSMC right now. Advanced packaging capacity has been repeatedly identified as a critical supply-chain vulnerability, especially for AI accelerators needing 2.5D/HBM integration.

These are real bottlenecks, just not investable as public pure-plays.

Macro Overlay

Regime for this theme: Risk-On / Neutral leaning Risk-On. But the easy money in semicap leaders is not guaranteed from here because positioning is crowded and valuations in the obvious names are not “hidden gem” anymore.

Trade Grade

Theme Grade: A- / 84

Not an automatic buy today — this is a watchlist construction map, not a blind entry signal. The best asymmetric hunting is likely in the smaller direct TSMC suppliers and CoWoS/materials names, not the already-mega-crowded semicap generals.

Position Size

For a portfolio basket, I’d cap initial exposure at:

Core semicap leaders: 3–5% total basket
Advanced packaging basket: 3–6%
Small Taiwan/Japan supplier basket: 2–4% max until liquidity/technicals confirm
Single small-cap supplier: usually ≤0.5–1.0% unless chart + fundamentals are both screaming
Portfolio Risk Check

Do not own ten companies that are all secretly the same trade. ASML + AMAT + LRCX + KLAC + TEL + DISCO + Advantest + Onto is basically one giant “semicap/TSMC capex stays hot” bet. Correlation risk is real. The market will treat them like cousins at a family reunion: maybe different personalities, same bloodline.

Biggest Risk to Thesis

The biggest risk is not “TSMC stops being important.” That is not the issue. The risk is capex digestion: if AI demand pauses, TSMC slows capacity adds, CoWoS supply catches up, or customers digest inventory, the supplier stocks can compress hard even while the long-term thesis remains intact. That is how good themes still punch you in the mouth.
