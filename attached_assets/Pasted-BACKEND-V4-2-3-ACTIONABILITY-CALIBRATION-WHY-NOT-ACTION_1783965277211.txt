BACKEND — V4.2.3 ACTIONABILITY CALIBRATION + “WHY NOT ACTIONABLE?” AUDIT

Backend only.

Do not touch frontend.
Do not change V4.2.1/V4.2.2 scoring weights.
Do not change Confluence score formula.
Do not change provider calls.
Do not call LLMs.
Do not rebuild options/catalyst/investment engines.

Goal:

V4.2.2 made Actionable and Near Actionable mutually exclusive, but it overcorrected. Actionable count is now 0 even though many watchlist names are clearly actionable setups.

We need to recalibrate the decision layer.

Confluence score = evidence strength.
Actionability = whether the current setup/entry is tradable now.

Do not require actionability_state to literally be READY/ACTIONABLE. That is too strict because the engine often emits NEAR_ACTIONABLE / WAIT_FOR_RETEST for setups that are actually buyable.

==================================================
PART 2 — ACTIONABLE SHOULD NOT REQUIRE READY STATE ONLY
==================================================

Current bad rule:

is_actionable_setup requires actionability_state in {READY, ACTIONABLE}

Replace with a setup/entry driven rule.

Hard exclusions:

If confluence_eligible = false:
  actionable_tier = INELIGIBLE
  is_actionable_setup = false
  is_near_actionable = false

If is_risk_conflict = true OR actionability_state in {AVOID, RISK_CONFLICT}:
  actionable_tier = RISK_CONFLICT
  is_actionable_setup = false
  is_near_actionable = false

If active support is confirmed lost / support_lost / major lower low confirmed:
  actionable_tier = NOT_ACTIONABLE
  blocker = SUPPORT_LOST or CONFIRMED_LOWER_LOW

Do not hard-block merely because:
- options_status = not_scanned
- catalyst_points = 0
- actionability_state = NEAR_ACTIONABLE
- actionability_state = WAIT_FOR_RETEST
- actionability_state = WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT

==================================================
PART 3 — ACTIONABLE NOW RULES
==================================================

Set is_actionable_setup = true when any of these paths pass.

PATH A — SUPPORT / PULLBACK ENTRY

Conditions:
- eligible
- no risk conflict
- stage_quality_points >= 9 / 15
- entry_exit_points >= 6.5 / 12
- technical_setup_points >= 3.5 / 8
- support intact OR testing active support OR near active support OR reclaim level nearby
- not confirmed support lost
- not chase extension with poor entry

Then:
actionable_tier = ACTIONABLE_NOW
actionable_path = support_entry or ema_pullback
positive reason = ACTIONABLE_SUPPORT_ENTRY

PATH B — RETEST ENTRY

Conditions:
- eligible
- no risk conflict
- actionability_state in {WAIT_FOR_RETEST, NEAR_ACTIONABLE}
- entry_exit_points >= 6.0 / 12
- technical_setup_points >= 4.0 / 8
- stage_quality_points >= 9 / 15
- support/retest level exists or breakout shelf/retest pattern exists

Then:
actionable_tier = ACTIONABLE_NOW
actionable_path = retest_entry
positive reason = ACTIONABLE_RETEST_ENTRY

Important:
WAIT_FOR_RETEST should not automatically mean not actionable.
If price is currently at or near retest/support, it can be actionable.

PATH C — BREAKOUT / CONTINUATION ENTRY

Conditions:
- eligible
- no risk conflict
- stage_quality_points >= 10.5 / 15
- technical_setup_points >= 5.5 / 8
- entry_exit_points >= 5.0 / 12
- setup label indicates:
  HIGH_TIGHT_FLAG
  BULL_FLAG
  BREAKOUT_SHELF
  VCP
  high-tight continuation
  bull flag
  breakout shelf
- not extreme/chase extension with poor R/R

Then:
actionable_tier = AGGRESSIVE_ACTIONABLE
actionable_path = breakout_entry or high_tight_continuation
positive reason = ACTIONABLE_CONTINUATION_SETUP

PATH D — LOW BASE / REVERSAL ENTRY

Conditions:
- eligible
- no risk conflict
- technical_setup_label indicates LOW_BASE_REVERSAL, support bounce, double-touch low, reversal watch
- entry_exit_points >= 6.5 / 12
- support intact
- no confirmed lower low
- no confirmed support lost

Then:
actionable_tier = ACTIONABLE_NOW
actionable_path = reversal_entry
positive reason = ACTIONABLE_REVERSAL_AT_SUPPORT

PATH E — HIGH CONFLUENCE EXCEPTION

Conditions:
- eligible
- no risk conflict
- caelyn_confluence_score >= 60
- entry_exit_points >= 5.5
- technical_setup_points >= 4.0
- stage_quality_points >= 9
- not support lost
- not extreme chase extension

Then:
actionable_tier = AGGRESSIVE_ACTIONABLE
positive reason = ACTIONABLE_HIGH_CONFLUENCE_ACCEPTABLE_ENTRY

==================================================
PART 4 — NEAR ACTIONABLE RULES
==================================================

Set is_near_actionable = true only when is_actionable_setup = false.

Near Actionable means:
good candidate, but needs one more thing.

Conditions:
- eligible
- no risk conflict
- not support lost
- score/setup quality is decent
- but one of:
  entry_exit_points between 4.5 and 6.5
  technical_setup_points between 3.0 and 5.0
  needs breakout trigger
  needs reclaim
  needs cleaner retest
  slightly extended from support

Then:
actionable_tier = NEAR_ACTIONABLE

Reason codes:
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_NEEDS_BREAKOUT
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_NEEDS_RECLAIM
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_NEEDS_RETEST
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_ENTRY_NOT_CLEAN
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_SETUP_GOOD_ENTRY_PENDING

==================================================
PART 5 — WATCH FOR RESET RULES
==================================================

Watch for Reset means:
good stock or theme, but current entry is not good.

Use when:
- eligible
- no hard risk conflict
- setup/confluence may be good
- but entry is too extended, support too far below, or R/R poor

Reason codes:
WATCH_FOR_RESET_EXTENDED
WATCH_FOR_RESET_SUPPORT_TOO_FAR
WATCH_FOR_RESET_RR_POOR
WATCH_FOR_RESET_CHASE_EXTENSION

Do not put clean support/EMA pullbacks here.
