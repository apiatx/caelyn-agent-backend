Goal:
Add Stage Analysis to the Themes page so every theme/sub-theme/sector can be categorized as Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, or Stage 4, including transition labels like Stage 1 → 2 breakout watch and Stage 3 → 4 danger zone.

Important stage rules:
- Use weekly charts, not intraday/day-trading logic.
- Core reference is the 30-week moving average.
- Stage 1 = basing after a decline, mostly sideways, price oscillating around a flat 30-week MA.
- Stage 1 → 2 = breakout from base, ideally above resistance, above/rising 30-week MA, with volume expansion.
- Stage 2 = advancing trend, price mostly above rising 30-week MA.
- Stage 3 = topping/consolidation, momentum fades, 30-week MA flattens, price oscillates around/near MA after prior uptrend.
- Stage 3 → 4 = danger zone / breakdown risk.
- Stage 4 = decline, price below declining 30-week MA.
- Relative strength vs SPY matters.
- Broad market stage matters.
- Volume confirmation matters most on Stage 1 → 2 breakouts.

Classification guidance:
- Stage 1 Base:
  - prior decline
  - price near/around 30-week MA
  - 30-week MA flat or flattening
  - range compression / sideways behavior
  - low or drying volume
- Stage 1 → 2 Breakout Watch:
  - stage 1 conditions
  - price near top of base / resistance
  - price crossing above 30-week MA or already above it
  - 30-week MA flattening to rising
  - improving RS vs SPY
  - volume starting to expand
- Stage 2 Advance:
  - price above rising 30-week MA
  - higher highs / higher lows
  - RS vs SPY rising or stable positive
  - healthy breadth across members
- Stage 3 Top/Range:
  - prior Stage 2 advance
  - 30-week MA flattening
  - price choppy around/above MA
  - RS weakening
  - breadth deteriorating
- Stage 3 → 4 Danger:
  - Stage 3 characteristics plus price breaking below support/30-week MA
  - falling RS
  - breadth deterioration
- Stage 4 Decline:
  - price below declining 30-week MA
  - lower highs/lower lows
  - weak RS
  - poor breadth
