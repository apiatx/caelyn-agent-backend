==================================================
PART 1 — DEFINE CLEAN BACKEND SEMANTICS
==================================================

Make the backend contract unambiguous:

caelyn_confluence_score:
Ranking score.

caelyn_confluence_bucket:
User-facing category. Must be actionability-aware.

actionability_state:
Decision layer. Answers what to do now.

A ticker must not have:

caelyn_confluence_bucket = ACTIONABLE

unless actionability_state is one of:

READY
NEAR_ACTIONABLE

or another explicitly approved actionable state.

If actionability_state = WATCH, then bucket cannot be ACTIONABLE.

If the model wants to preserve high-score-but-not-actionable names, use a bucket like:

HIGH_CONFLUENCE_WATCH
HIGH_CONFLUENCE_NOT_ACTIONABLE
INVESTMENT_QUALITY
WATCH_FOR_RESET
NO_CLEAR_CONFLUENCE

Pick the cleanest existing-compatible label.

Do not leave contradictory labels.

PART 4 — CLEAN LEGACY ACTIONABILITY DISPLAY
==================================================

Do not let frontend display mixed strings.
