BACKEND — V4.2.2 ACTIONABILITY + INVESTMENT QUALITY CALIBRATION

Backend only.

Do not touch frontend.
Do not change provider calls.
Do not call LLMs.
Do not change core V4.2.1 weights.
Do not change options/catalyst/scanner infrastructure.
Do not rebuild the scoring engine.

Goal:

Fix the decision semantics exposed by the frontend:

1. Actionable and Near Actionable are currently not distinct enough.
2. Investment Quality is too binary because it only uses 0/1/2/3 strong pillar count.
3. Foreign/OTC/prefixed tickers need explicit confluence eligibility fields.

==================================================
PART 1 — CONFLUENCE ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

Add fields on every alignment row:

confluence_eligible
confluence_ineligible_reason

Rule:

If symbol contains ":":
  confluence_eligible = false
  confluence_ineligible_reason = "prefixed_foreign_or_otc_symbol"

Else:
  confluence_eligible = true
  confluence_ineligible_reason = null

Do not remove these rows from backend response yet.
Frontend will filter them.

Examples:
MSFT → eligible
NVDA → eligible
TSM → eligible
OTC:ATEYY → not eligible
AIM:VLX → not eligible
AMS:BESI → not eligible

==================================================
PART 2 — ACTIONABLE VS NEAR ACTIONABLE CLEANUP
==================================================

Make Actionable and Near Actionable distinct.

Definitions:

Actionable = can act now.
Near Actionable = good candidate, but needs one more thing.

Update boolean rules:

is_actionable_setup should be true only when:
- confluence_eligible = true
- no risk conflict
- actionability_state in {"READY", "ACTIONABLE"}
- entry_exit_points >= 8.0 out of 12
- technical_setup_points >= 5.0 out of 8
- not WATCH_FOR_RESET
- not WAIT_FOR_RETEST
- not WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT
- not AVOID
- not RISK_CONFLICT

is_near_actionable should be true when:
- confluence_eligible = true
- no risk conflict
- is_actionable_setup = false
- actionability_state in {"NEAR_ACTIONABLE", "WAIT_FOR_RETEST", "WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT"}
  OR caelyn_confluence_score >= 55 and entry_exit_points >= 5.5 and technical_setup_points >= 4.0

Make these mutually exclusive:

If is_actionable_setup = true:
  is_near_actionable = false

Keep:
is_watch_for_reset
is_risk_conflict
is_investment_quality

But ensure prefixed foreign/OTC symbols are false for all decision tabs.

Expose reason codes:

ACTIONABLE_READY_NOW
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_NEEDS_RETEST
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_NEEDS_BREAKOUT
NEAR_ACTIONABLE_ENTRY_NOT_CLEAN
NOT_ACTIONABLE_ENTRY_TOO_WEAK
NOT_ACTIONABLE_RISK_CONFLICT
NOT_ACTIONABLE_INELIGIBLE_SYMBOL

Return before/after counts:

Actionable count
Near Actionable count
Overlap count

Expected:
Overlap count = 0

==================================================
PART 3 — INVESTMENT QUALITY CONTINUOUS RANKING
==================================================

Keep the existing 3-pillar model, but add continuous ranking scores.

Current model:
0 pillars = 0 points
1 pillar = 5
2 pillars = 10
3 pillars = 15

That can remain for investment_alignment_points if needed.

But Investment Quality tab needs ranked scores.

Add:

financial_health_score_0_100
current_growth_score_0_100
forward_growth_score_0_100
investment_quality_score
investment_quality_rank_label
investment_quality_reason_codes

Each pillar should be ranked from 0–100 using the existing FMP fields.

Financial Health inputs:
- Gross Mgn
- FCF Mgn
- Free CF
- Op. Income
- EBIT
- D/E
- ND/EBITDA

Suggested scoring:
- Gross Mgn strength: up to 20
- FCF Mgn strength: up to 20
- Free CF positive: up to 15
- Op. Income positive: up to 15
- EBIT positive: up to 10
- D/E manageable: up to 10
- ND/EBITDA manageable: up to 10

Current Growth inputs:
- Rev Grwth Q
- Rev Grwth Y
- EPS Grwth
- EPS Grwth TQ/TY
- FCF Mgn
- Op. Income
- EBIT

Suggested scoring:
- Rev Grwth Q strength: up to 25
- Rev Grwth Y strength: up to 20
- EPS growth strength: up to 20
- profitability positive: up to 20
- FCF/margin quality: up to 15

Forward Growth inputs:
- Rev Grwth Est
- Rev Grwth NQ
- Rev Grwth NY
- EPS Grwth Est
- EPS Grwth NQ
- EPS Grwth NY

Suggested scoring:
- Rev Grwth Est strength: up to 25
- Rev Grwth NQ strength: up to 25
- Rev Grwth NY strength: up to 20
- Forward EPS growth strength: up to 20
- Hypergrowth/speculative revenue rule: up to 10

Final Investment Quality score:

best_pillar = max(financial_health_score_0_100, current_growth_score_0_100, forward_growth_score_0_100)

breadth_bonus:
0 strong pillars = 0
1 strong pillar = 0
2 strong pillars = +7
3 strong pillars = +12

investment_quality_score = min(100, best_pillar + breadth_bonus)

Labels:
90–100 = A+ Investment
80–89 = Excellent Investment Quality
70–79 = Strong Investment Quality
60–69 = Watchlist Quality
40–59 = Mixed Investment Quality
0–39 = Weak / No Clear Investment Case

Important:
Do not punish speculative forward growth names for weak current profitability if forward growth score is high.
Do not let valuation fields kill the score.
P/E, P/S, EV/EBITDA remain context/warnings only.

Expose both:
investment_alignment_points
investment_quality_score

The Investment Quality tab should sort by investment_quality_score.
