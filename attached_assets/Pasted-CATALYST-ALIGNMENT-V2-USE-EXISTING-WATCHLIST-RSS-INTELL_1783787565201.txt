==================================================
PRODUCT OBJECTIVE
=================

Catalyst Alignment should answer:

"Has something happened, or is something scheduled, that can materially change this ticker's business trajectory, demand, market position, investor expectations, or near-term setup quality?"

Do not score "news volume."

Do not reward routine articles.

Do not average compelling events down because boring articles also exist.

A single major event can be the catalyst.

Examples:

CRDO:
major hyperscaler design win / production award / strategic AI infrastructure customer

AEVA:
OEM production award / series production / customer qualification

IBRX:
FDA milestone / Phase 3 endpoint / PDUFA / approval / clinical hold

RCAT / defense names:
DoD contract / program award / military procurement

materials/mining:
offtake agreement / permit / commissioning / first production

early revenue companies:
commercial launch / first shipment / mass production / capacity ramp

software:
major enterprise agreement / GA launch / strategic platform partnership

Speculative companies:
non-dilutive funding is bullish
dilutive offering / going concern is bearish

PART 4 — MATERIALITY
====================

Do not use a plain weighted average across all articles.

Catalyst V2 should identify:

primary_positive_catalyst

supporting_positive_catalysts

primary_bearish_catalyst

materiality/conflict penalty

Do not let routine articles dilute a major event.

Do not let many duplicated syndicated articles count as many independent catalysts.

Use an event-clustering/dedupe step.

Simple V1 event dedupe is acceptable:

same ticker

same event_type

normalized title similarity / shared URL / same day

same major entity mention if available

Collapse duplicate articles into one event with article_count/source_count.

Article count may boost confidence slightly, not create multiple catalysts.

Recommended scoring concept:

positive_score =
primary_positive_event_score

* small corroboration_bonus capped at +8

negative_penalty =
primary_bearish_event_score * penalty factor

final_v2_score =
clamp(positive_score - negative_penalty, 0, 100)

If no meaningful positive or negative catalyst:
available = false
score = null

Do not score routine news as a catalyst.
