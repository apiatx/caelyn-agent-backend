Remaining concern:
Rows like AMD, AAOI, KLAC, TXN, TEL, TER are now served from `portfolio_opts_lkg_disk` with `stale=True`. That is fine only if the data is recent. It is not acceptable if the data is hours/days old.

Desired policy:

1. Quotes:

   * target freshness: 30–60 seconds
   * max acceptable: 2–5 minutes

2. Watchlist options signals:

   * target freshness: 5–15 minutes
   * absolute max stale age during market hours: 60 minutes

3. LKG fallback:

   * may be served during restart/rate-limit/temporary failures
   * must be immediately queued for refresh
   * should not remain stale longer than 60 minutes during market hours unless Tradier is failing repeatedly
   * if over 60 minutes, mark `stale_over_sla=true` and queue high-priority refresh
