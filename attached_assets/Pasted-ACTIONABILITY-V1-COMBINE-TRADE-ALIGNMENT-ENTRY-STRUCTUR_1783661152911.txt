PART 6 — EARLY_WATCH
====================

EARLY_WATCH is strategically important.

Intent:

A strong or improving opportunity is developing before the move is obvious, but current structure is not fully actionable yet.

Potential contexts:

HIGH_BASE_FORMING

early HIGH_BASE_COILING

BASE_FORMING

strong pre-move family states

CONSTRUCTIVE_DIP before trigger

other canonical forming states

Trade Alignment should be meaningfully positive.

Options pressure may be improving/accelerating.

Theme may be strong.

The stock should not be severely extended.

Recommended conceptual gate:

Trade Alignment >= 65

Entry in a forming/pre-move state

Entry Score roughly >= 45

no structural break

no severe extension

Do not require Options Alignment.

A stock may be EARLY_WATCH based on Theme + Stage + Catalyst.

Return reasons such as:

OPPORTUNITY_FORMING

PRE_MOVE_STRUCTURE

TRADE_ALIGNMENT_BUILDING

PREMIUM_PRESSURE_ACCELERATING

THEME_ALIGNMENT_STRONG

Use only real existing signals.

==================================================
PART 7 — WATCH
==============

WATCH is the safe neutral/developing state.

Use when:

Trade Alignment is valid but not strong enough for a higher-conviction state

or:

Entry is structurally neutral/unclear

or:

minimum evidence exists but opportunity/entry interaction is not mature

or:

Trade Alignment has insufficient evidence but the row remains tracked

Do not use WATCH as a dumping ground for broken structures.

Broken structures belong in AVOID.

Severe chase structures belong in TOO_EXTENDED / WAIT_FOR_RETEST.

WATCH should mean:

"Interesting or tracked, but no clear action now."

==================================================
PART 8 — WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT
==========================

Intent:

Opportunity alignment is meaningful.

A base/coiling structure exists.

Price has not yet cleared a defined ceiling/pivot/reclaim trigger.

Potential canonical contexts:

HIGH_BASE_COILING

HIGH_BASE_READY where the actual trigger has not fired and current Entry semantics indicate waiting

COILED

BASE_FORMING with mature resistance

other exact pre-breakout states

LOW_BASE_COILING

possibly LOW_BASE_READY if the model indicates an untriggered reclaim rather than immediate actionability

Do not create an artificial breakout calculation.

Reuse Entry Structure outputs:

breakout pivot

base ceiling

reclaim pivot

Entry reason codes

Entry state

Use:

WAIT_FOR_BREAKOUT

when the best action is:

"Do not chase inside the structure; wait for the trigger."

==================================================
PART 9 — WAIT_FOR_RETEST
========================

Intent:

The stock/opportunity remains strongly aligned.

Price has already expanded or broken out.

The current location is not attractive enough to initiate/add.

The setup needs:

retest

pullback

reset

or consolidation

Potential contexts:

WAIT_FOR_RETEST Entry state

BREAKOUT_CONFIRMED with weak current Entry score / extension conflict

EXTENDED when Trade Alignment remains strong

post-breakout location above support but poor risk/reward

Recommended conceptual requirement:

Trade Alignment meaningfully positive

structure not broken

current Entry conflict is primarily location/chase risk

Do not use WAIT_FOR_RETEST for SUPPORT_LOST.

==================================================
PART 10 — REVERSAL_WATCH
========================

Intent:

A deep-reset / turnaround structure is developing.

Downside is becoming defined.

Momentum is not yet sufficiently proven for READY.

Primary contexts:

LOW_BASE_FORMING

LOW_BASE_COILING where confirmation remains immature

REVERSAL_WATCH Entry state

BEARISH_EASING Options pressure may be a confirming reason

BULLISH_ACCELERATING Options pressure may be a stronger confirming reason

Trade Alignment does not need to be as high as READY because Stage may naturally be weak during an early turnaround.

However:

Do not surface every LOW_BASE_FORMING ticker as REVERSAL_WATCH regardless of opportunity context.

Require meaningful evidence from Trade Alignment and/or specific improving signals.

Recommended conceptual logic:

LOW_BASE archetype/state

AND

no structural-break conflict

AND

at least moderate Trade Alignment

or:

strong Options pressure improvement

or:

strong Theme/Catalyst confirmation

Use available real signals only.

Reason codes may include:

LOW_BASE_FLOOR_DEFINED

DOWNSIDE_DEFINED

MOMENTUM_UNPROVEN

BEARISH_PRESSURE_EASING

PREMIUM_PRESSURE_ACCELERATING

TURNAROUND_STRUCTURE_FORMING
