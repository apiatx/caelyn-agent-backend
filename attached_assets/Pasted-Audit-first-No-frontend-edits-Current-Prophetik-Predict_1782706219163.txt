Part C — Rebuild Market Read logic

Market Read must be generated from outcome-aware logic.

Inputs:

* family_key
* provider
* question
* most_likely_outcome label
* most_likely_probability
* priced_probability
* price_change_24h
* price_change_7d
* outcome_summary
* contract_context
* category

General formula:
market_read = scenario + momentum + investment interpretation

Examples:

Fed cuts:

* Most likely 0 cuts at 78%, rising:
  market_read: “higher-for-longer”
  interpretation: restrictive rates / bearish duration / pressure on high-multiple growth, REITs, speculative tech; supportive for cash, financials/NIM depending curve.
* More cuts rising:
  market_read: “rates easing”
  interpretation: bullish duration/growth/REITs, bearish dollar/financial NIM maybe.

Fed hike:

* Hike odds rising:
  market_read: “rates restrictive”
* Hike odds falling:
  market_read: “policy risk easing”

Fed no-change:

* No-change high and rising:
  market_read: “policy hold / higher-for-longer”
* Cut/increase outcome rising:
  reflect direction accordingly.

Recession:

* Recession odds high/rising:
  “growth stress rising”
* Recession odds low/falling:
  “soft landing / growth resilient”

Unemployment:

* Higher unemployment bucket most likely/rising:
  “labor weakening”
* Lower/stable unemployment most likely:
  “labor resilient”

SPX/Nasdaq daily up:

* Up probability high/rising:
  “risk-on / broad market bid”
* Up probability falling:
  “risk appetite fading”

S&P year-end / high ladder:

* Higher strike odds rising:
  “bullish index repricing”
* Lower range most likely or high strikes falling:
  “index upside fading”

S&P vs Gold:

* S&P winning probability high/rising:
  “risk assets outperforming safe haven”
* Gold winning probability high/rising:
  “safe haven / hard asset leadership”

Oil:

* Higher oil outcome rising:
  “energy inflation pressure”
* Higher oil outcome falling:
  “oil pressure easing”

Gold:

* Higher gold outcome rising:
  “safe-haven / real-rate hedge bid”
* Higher gold outcome falling:
  “risk-on / safe-haven bid fading”

BTC:

* Higher BTC outcome rising:
  “crypto risk-on”
* Falling:
  “crypto risk appetite fading”

Geopolitics:

* Stress/escalation outcome high/rising:
  “geopolitical stress rising”
* De-escalation outcome high/rising:
  “geopolitical stress easing”

Company/AI:

* NVDA/AI positive outcome high/rising:
  “AI leadership strengthening”
* Falling:
  “AI leadership premium fading”

Part D — Exposure must use the Market Read scenario

Exposure should be based on:

* most likely outcome
* whether that scenario is becoming more or less likely
* investment logic

Examples:

* higher-for-longer:
  bearish: REITs, long-duration growth, speculative high beta
  mixed/positive: financials/cash-like yield, quality balance sheets
* rates easing:
  bullish: growth, software, semis, REITs, housing
  bearish/mixed: banks/NIM depending curve
* risk-on index:
  bullish: broad market, semis, software, cyclicals
  bearish relative: defensives/safe haven
* recession risk rising:
  bullish relative: gold/safe haven/defensives
  bearish: cyclicals, financials, high beta
* oil pressure rising:
  bullish: energy
  bearish: airlines/transports/consumer discretionary margins
* gold bid rising:
  bullish: gold/miners/safe haven
  bearish relative: high-beta risk assets
* S&P outperforming gold:
  bullish: broad equities/cyclicals
  bearish relative: gold/safe haven
* gold outperforming S&P:
  bullish: gold/miners/defensives
  bearish: broad risk/cyclicals
