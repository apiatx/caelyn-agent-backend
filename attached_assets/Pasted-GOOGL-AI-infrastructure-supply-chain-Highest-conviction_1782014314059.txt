Regime: Neutral-to-Risk-On, but crowded.

Biggest Risk to Thesis

The biggest risk is not “AI goes away.” That’s lazy.

The real risks:

Google slows capex growth after a digestion cycle.
TPU economics disappoint versus Nvidia GPU clusters.
Supplier margins get competed down as Google dual-sources.
TSMC/HBM/CoWoS constraints ease, killing scarcity premium.
Power interconnection delays push data-center timelines out.
China/Taiwan geopolitical risk hits the Asian supplier stack.
Crowding risk: the thesis is right, but everyone already owns it.
Final take

The best GOOGL public bottleneck names by purity are:

AVGO, TSM, SK Hynix, Samsung, MU, ASML, Envicool, ETN, VRT, Schneider, Foxconn, NEE, AES, Inventec, Innolight, LITE, COHR, AMKR, ASE, Unimicron, Ibiden, Victory Giant, Eoptolink, Auras, Delta Electronics, CRDO, FN, EMCOR, FIX, POWL.

The most asymmetric hunting ground is probably not the mega-caps anymore. It’s the small/mid-cap supply chain around cooling, substrates, PCBs, optical modules, power gear, and construction capacity. That’s where the market still sometimes looks at a mission-critical bottleneck and says, “cool little industrial company,” like an idiot with a Bloomberg terminal.
