# Objective

Implement the finalized canonical model:

```text
One actual economic sector
+
One primary thematic membership
+
Zero or more additional thematic memberships
```

A thematic membership can point directly to:

* a parent theme, or
* a subtheme

The user-facing hierarchy must remain at a maximum of:

```text
Sector → Theme → Subtheme
```

Do not create subthemes beneath subthemes.

A company may belong directly to a parent theme when no narrower subtheme fits.

A company may have meaningful memberships across multiple theme families.

Example:

```text
ALAB

Actual sector:
Technology

Primary:
Semiconductors
  → Data Center Connectivity & Interconnect Silicon

Additional:
Data Center Infrastructure
  → Networking & Fabric Infrastructure
```

Do not store redundant parent memberships when a stored child already rolls up to that parent.

Selecting a parent in the frontend must continue to find:

* direct parent members
* every member of its children

Selecting a child must remain exact.

Sector filtering must continue to include:

* companies whose actual sector matches
* companies whose thematic memberships roll up to that sector
