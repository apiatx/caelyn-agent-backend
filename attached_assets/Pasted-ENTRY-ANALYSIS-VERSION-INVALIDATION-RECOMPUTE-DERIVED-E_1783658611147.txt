Exact root cause already proven:

==================================================
ARCHITECTURAL DISTINCTION
=========================

There are two separate freshness concepts:

1. MARKET DATA FRESHNESS

Are the canonical daily bars / Stage history usable according to the existing Stage architecture?

2. DERIVED ENTRY ANALYSIS FRESHNESS

Was the cached Entry state computed using the current Entry model semantics/schema?

These are NOT the same.

A ticker may have:

usable current cached daily bars

but:

an Entry row computed before LOW_BASE states existed

In that case:

RECOMPUTE ENTRY LOCALLY FROM EXISTING BARS

Do not automatically refetch market history.
