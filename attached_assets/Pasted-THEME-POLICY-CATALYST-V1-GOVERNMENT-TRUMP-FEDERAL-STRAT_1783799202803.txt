Backend only.

No provider calls.

Use only existing cached/persisted news/RSS/calendar/policy data already available in the app.

Goal:

Add a deterministic Theme Policy Catalyst layer that detects government / Trump / White House / federal strategic investment and industrial-policy announcements that can lift an entire theme, not just one ticker.

This is separate from ticker-specific Catalyst V2.

Ticker Catalyst V2 answers:

“Did something happen for this company?”

“Did something happen that should boost this entire theme or industry?”

Examples:

rare earths / critical minerals strategic investment

defense procurement theme
gold/silver reserve accumulation
tariffs/export controls favoring domestic suppliers

==================================================
PART 4 — APPLY TO TICKERS
==================================================

For each ticker, calculate theme_policy_boost only if:

ticker primary_theme matches affected theme

or ticker additional theme membership matches affected theme

or ticker industry/description strongly matches affected theme

Do not boost unrelated tickers.

Boost should depend on theme fit:

primary theme exact match:
full boost

additional theme match:
partial boost

sector/industry-only weak match:
small boost

Suggested cap:

theme_policy_boost max +15 to Catalyst score

or expose separately first as shadow field:

theme_policy_boost_score
theme_policy_event
theme_policy_relevance

Do not let theme policy alone create a BUY.

It should improve Catalyst Alignment / Trade Alignment, but Stage/Options/Theme/Entry still matter.

==================================================
PART 5 — SCORING
==================================================

Keep ticker-specific Catalyst V2 intact.

Compute:

ticker_catalyst_score = existing promoted Catalyst score

theme_policy_score = 0–100 internal

ticker_theme_relevance = 0.0–1.0

theme_policy_boost = min(15, theme_policy_score * ticker_theme_relevance * 0.15)

combined_catalyst_score =
clamp(ticker_catalyst_score + theme_policy_boost - bearish_penalty, 0, 100)

If no ticker catalyst exists but theme_policy_boost exists:

combined_catalyst_score should become available only if boost is meaningful, e.g. >= 8.

But mark primary_source = "theme_policy", not "rss_v2".

Do not hide that it is theme-level, not company-specific.
