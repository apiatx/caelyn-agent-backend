What Amazon Is Most Exposed To
1. Power is the scariest bottleneck

Amazon can buy servers faster than utilities can build interconnection capacity. The highest-quality physical choke points are Talen, GE Vernova, AEP/Bloom, Schneider, Eaton, Vertiv, Powell, Hammond, Quanta Services, EMCOR.

2. Nvidia is still unavoidable

Trainium helps Amazon reduce Nvidia dependence, but it does not eliminate the need for Nvidia GPUs, HBM, CoWoS, and AI server manufacturing. AWS P6 is Nvidia-based, and AWS is still building around Nvidia for top-end AI workloads.

3. Custom silicon creates a second supply chain

Trainium makes Alchip, Marvell, TSMC, Wiwynn, memory suppliers, and packaging suppliers strategically more important. This is where smaller public names can hide.

4. Corning is the cleanest direct fiber winner

The Corning-Amazon agreement is exactly the type of direct bottleneck signal you want: multiyear, multibillion-dollar, tied to AWS data-center expansion.

5. Retail/logistics matters, but it is lower asymmetry

Rivian and Sun Country are real direct Amazon suppliers, but logistics is more replaceable than AI compute/power. Packaging is even more replaceable.

Biggest Risk to the Thesis

The biggest risk is overpaying for obvious AI-infrastructure winners after the market already crowned them kings. Bottleneck quality does not automatically equal stock upside. A company can be critical and still be a bad trade if valuation, chart, and expectations are stretched.

Portfolio Risk Check

I would not buy this as a giant basket. Too much correlation. The clean approach is:

Core bottlenecks: 3–5 names max
Speculative smaller bottlenecks: 2–4 names max
Wait for technical setups: no breakout/pullback/invalidation = no trade
Avoid duplicate exposure: Schneider + Eaton + Vertiv + Powell + Hammond all move on the same data-center power theme. Great theme, but don’t accidentally buy the same horse five times wearing different hats.

Final Take

For Amazon specifically, the best bottleneck stack is:

TLN / GEV / GLW / NVDA / TSM / MRVL / Alchip / Wiwynn / SK Hynix / ETN / VRT / Schneider / BE / AEP / POWL / HPS.A / CRDO / ALAB / FN / RIVN / SNCY.

The highest-upside hunting ground is probably not the mega-caps everyone already owns. It is the second-order layer: custom ASIC design, AI server ODMs, power equipment, transformers/switchgear, thermal management, and optical connectivity. That is where Amazon’s capex becomes someone else’s revenue ramp.
