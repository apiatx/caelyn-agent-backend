PART G — CALCULATE 24H NEWS ACTIVITY

For a requested Watchlist's current ticker set, calculate per ticker:

articles_24h
previous_articles_24h
delta_count
delta_pct

Definitions:

articles_24h =
count of unique RSS article associations where:
published_at >= now - 24 hours
AND published_at <= now

previous_articles_24h =
count of unique RSS article associations where:
published_at >= now - 48 hours
AND published_at < now - 24 hours

delta_count =
articles_24h - previous_articles_24h

delta_pct:

if previous_articles_24h > 0:
((articles_24h - previous_articles_24h) / previous_articles_24h) * 100

if previous_articles_24h == 0 and articles_24h == 0:
0.0

if previous_articles_24h == 0 and articles_24h > 0:
null

Also provide:

delta_label = "new"

for the final case.

Do not fabricate +100%, +500%, or another percent when the prior denominator is zero.
