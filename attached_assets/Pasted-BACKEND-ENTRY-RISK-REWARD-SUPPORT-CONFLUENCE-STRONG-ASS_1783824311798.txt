PART 2 — ADD ENTRY RISK/REWARD STATE
==================================================

Add derived fields to Entry / Confluence output:

entry_risk_reward_state
entry_risk_reward_score
entry_risk_reward_reason_codes

Suggested states:

ASYMMETRIC_SUPPORT_ENTRY
SUPPORT_TEST_CONFIRMING
SUPPORT_TEST_NEEDS_CONFIRMATION
LOW_BASE_RISK_DEFINED
PULLBACK_TO_SUPPORT
STRONG_ASSET_EXTENDED_WAIT
BROKEN_SUPPORT_AVOID
NO_CLEAR_ENTRY

Rules:

ASYMMETRIC_SUPPORT_ENTRY requires:

investment_alignment_score >= 70 if available
active_support_status in above_support / testing_support / bounced_from_support
price near active support zone, preferably within +0% to +8% above zone
lower_low_confirmed is false
extension_state is not EXTREME_EXTENSION / VERTICAL / CROWDED_MOVE
entry_state is not SUPPORT_LOST / LOWER_LOW_CONFIRMED / FAILED_BREAKOUT
some consolidation/base/support evidence exists:
  repeated support touches
  base_archetype LOW_BASE or HIGH_BASE
  active_support_type double_bounce_support / base_low / trendline_support / range_low

SUPPORT_TEST_CONFIRMING:

active support is being tested or recently bounced
trade_alignment_score >= 55
lower_low_confirmed false
needs reclaim / bounce confirmation

SUPPORT_TEST_NEEDS_CONFIRMATION:

support nearby but trade/investment quality is not strong enough yet
or no confirmation

LOW_BASE_RISK_DEFINED:

LOW_BASE_FORMING / REVERSAL_WATCH with clear critical_break_level nearby
but not enough confirmation for READY

STRONG_ASSET_EXTENDED_WAIT:

investment_alignment_score >= 70
but extension_state is EXTREME_EXTENSION / VERTICAL / CROWDED_MOVE
or distance from active support is too far

BROKEN_SUPPORT_AVOID:

active_support_status = lost_confirmed
or lower_low_confirmed = true

==================================================
PART 3 — ENTRY SCORE INTEGRATION
==================================================

Use entry_risk_reward_state to influence entry_score.

Do not blindly override existing Entry V2.

Additive adjustment only:

ASYMMETRIC_SUPPORT_ENTRY:
raise/support entry_score into strong zone, e.g. 70–85, unless other hard risk exists

SUPPORT_TEST_CONFIRMING:
entry_score 60–75 depending on confirmation

SUPPORT_TEST_NEEDS_CONFIRMATION:
entry_score 45–60

LOW_BASE_RISK_DEFINED:
entry_score 45–65

STRONG_ASSET_EXTENDED_WAIT:
entry_score capped below actionable threshold, e.g. max 45

BROKEN_SUPPORT_AVOID:
entry_score capped very low, e.g. max 20

Do not make support proximity alone a BUY.

Actionability still requires Trade Alignment and confirmation.

==================================================
PART 4 — ACTIONABILITY INTEGRATION
==================================================

Actionability should understand the new Entry risk/reward states.

If:

entry_risk_reward_state = ASYMMETRIC_SUPPORT_ENTRY
and trade_alignment_score >= threshold
and no lower low / no extreme extension

then actionability can be:

READY
or WATCH/WAIT_FOR_RECLAIM depending on confirmation.

If:

SUPPORT_TEST_CONFIRMING

then:

WATCH / WAIT_FOR_RECLAIM / WAIT_FOR_BOUNCE_CONFIRMATION

If:

STRONG_ASSET_EXTENDED_WAIT

then:

TOO_EXTENDED / WAIT_FOR_RESET

If:

BROKEN_SUPPORT_AVOID

then:

AVOID

Do not let Options Asymmetric RR override Entry.
