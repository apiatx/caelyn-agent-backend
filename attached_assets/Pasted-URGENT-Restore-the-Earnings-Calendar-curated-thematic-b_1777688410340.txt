URGENT: Restore the Earnings Calendar curated thematic behavior. Audit first, then fix only after finding the root cause.

Context:
The Earnings Calendar page used to work correctly.

Historical intended behavior:
- The page originally had a Curated / All toggle.
- “All” was removed because it caused too many FMP calls / rate-limit issues.
- The page was intentionally changed to Curated-only.
- Curated did NOT mean megacaps only.
- Curated meant the highest-quality, most relevant earnings for each day based on the app’s custom thematic / relevance logic.
- Rows were marked “Focus” for top-priority earnings.
- The calendar showed meaningful curated earnings across days.

- It did not restrict earnings candidates to ETF holdings / theme proxy baskets.

Current regression:
After recent theme universe / thematic context work, the Earnings Calendar is much weaker:
- mostly megacaps
- fewer earnings events
- some days blank
- “Focus” behavior appears degraded or missing
- it appears the candidate universe may be restricted to theme ETF basket/proxy names

This must be fixed.

Do NOT restore the old “All earnings” view.
Do NOT show every FMP earnings event.
Do NOT re-add the Curated / All toggle unless absolutely necessary.
Restore the old curated-only behavior.

Thematic universe / ETF baskets may help score or label earnings, but must NOT be the only candidate universe.

FMP earnings calendar / existing earnings snapshot source
→ broad earnings event candidates for the requested date range

→ optional theme/regime annotations

Incorrect flow:
THEME_RS_UNIVERSE / ETF holdings / proxy_symbols
