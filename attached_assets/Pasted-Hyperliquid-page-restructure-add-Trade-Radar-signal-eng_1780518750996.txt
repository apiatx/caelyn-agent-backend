Signal scoring v1:
- Keep this explainable and deterministic.
- No ML.
- Reuse existing per-asset metrics already calculated for Market Matrix and current widgets.
- Long setup should reward:
  - positive price momentum
  - positive mark/oracle delta
  - positive premium
  - strong trade flow
  - constructive OI
  - volume velocity
  - funding not too extreme
  - low/medium risk
- Penalize:
  - extreme funding
  - crowded/cap risk
  - weak volume
  - OI not confirming
  - overextension
  - high risk score
- Short setup should mirror the same logic.
- Use WATCH_BREAKOUT or BUY_PULLBACK when setup is good but not immediately actionable.
- Use CROWDED_AVOID when funding/crowding/risk are too high.
- TRADE_NOW should be rare and require high score/confidence and non-high risk.

Plain-English explanation rules:
- Do not say “will go up” or “guaranteed.”
- Use language like:
  - “bias”
  - “setup”
  - “watch”
  - “trigger”
  - “risk”
  - “wait for confirmation”
  - “avoid chase”
- Every SetupCard needs why_now, entry_condition, invalidation_hint, and warnings.
