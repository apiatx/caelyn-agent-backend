Classification rules:

1. For each contract with volume > 0:

   * determine mid = (bid + ask) / 2 when bid/ask are valid

* last >= ask, or within a small tolerance of ask:
  ask_side
* last <= bid, or within a small tolerance of bid:
  bid_side
* last between bid and ask:
  midpoint_or_unknown

Do not force midpoint/unknown contracts into bid or ask.

Do not classify purely from option type.
