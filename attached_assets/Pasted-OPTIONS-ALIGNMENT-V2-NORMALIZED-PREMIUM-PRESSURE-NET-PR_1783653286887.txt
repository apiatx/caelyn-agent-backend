## LONG-BIASED SCORE SEMANTICS

This score is for long-biased swing Trade Alignment.

Therefore:

strong bullish normalized premium pressure
→ high score

mixed / balanced pressure
→ middle score

strong bearish normalized premium pressure
→ low score

Do not convert absolute magnitude into bullishness.

Direction and imbalance determine bullish/bearish score.

Magnitude/relative intensity may strengthen confidence.

---

or premium imbalance.
