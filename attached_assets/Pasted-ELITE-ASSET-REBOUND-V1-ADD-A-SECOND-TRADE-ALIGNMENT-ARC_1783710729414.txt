PRODUCT DEFINITION
==================

ELITE_ASSET_REBOUND is for stocks that:

1. Have strong or elite Investment Alignment.

2. Previously demonstrated meaningful longer-term leadership or strong asset quality.

3. Experienced a meaningful reset, decline, pullback, or loss of prior momentum.

4. Still retain a strong underlying investment trajectory.

5. Are beginning to show structural rebound / reversal / rebuilding evidence.

6. May show improving Options premium pressure where data exists.

This is NOT:

"buy every high-quality stock after a decline"

This is NOT:

"value investing"

"fallen angel because price is down"

This is NOT:

a replacement for THEME_ALIGNMENT

Actionability
