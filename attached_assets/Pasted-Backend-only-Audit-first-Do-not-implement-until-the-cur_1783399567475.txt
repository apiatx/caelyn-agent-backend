Backend only. Audit first. Do not implement until the current Net Flow persistence and daily semantics are fully traced.

New feature goal:
Options Flow → Net Flow needs historical Net Premium direction/change.

For every ticker, theme, and sector expose:
- net_premium_delta_1d
- net_premium_delta_7d
- net_premium_delta_30d

These should show whether Net Premium is becoming more positive, less positive, more negative, or less negative over time.

Example:

Today Net Premium = +48M
Previous trading session = +12M

1D Δ = +36M

Another example:

Today = +48M
Previous trading session = +110M

1D Δ = -62M

Important:
Use prior AVAILABLE TRADING SESSIONS, not calendar-day subtraction.

Definitions:
- 1D = previous completed/available trading session
- 7D = 7 prior trading sessions
- 30D = 30 prior trading sessions
